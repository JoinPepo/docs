Pepo watches social video for your brand — your posts, your competitors', and the far larger volume of content where people discuss you without tagging you — and answers questions about it in plain language.

You ask from Claude or ChatGPT. Or you call the API and build it into your own product.

```
Which of our videos is actually taking off this week —
and is that a real trend, or one creator having a moment?
```

## What makes the answers usable

**You can see what's behind every number.** A momentum score arrives with how many observations it rests on, how many hours they span, and how many comparable videos it was ranked against. When someone asks where a number came from, you can answer.

**Size and momentum are different questions.** A video with a lot of views is a big video. Whether something is *happening* is a claim about trajectory, and Pepo scores each video against genuinely comparable ones and against the creator's own normal — so a 40k-view video from a small creator can outrank an 800k-view video from a mega-account. Usually that smaller one is the one worth acting on.

**One video ≠ a trend.** The cluster verdict is reported separately from the ranking, so you know whether you're looking at one creator having a big week or a format multiple independent creators are picking up.

**It tells you when it doesn't know.** A brand-new workspace says so rather than scoring off a single observation. That's the difference between a number you can take into a meeting and one you'll get caught on.
