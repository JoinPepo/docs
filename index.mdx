Pepo answers questions about what is happening to a brand on social video — what is growing, who is talking, what consumers actually say — and returns the evidence alongside the answer.

There are two ways in, and they are for different people.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="MCP" icon="plug" href="/mcp/overview">
    Connect Pepo to Claude or ChatGPT and ask in plain language. For marketing and insights teams. No code.
  </Card>
  <Card title="REST API" icon="code" href="/quickstart">
    The same workflows over HTTP, for your own product or internal tooling.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

The MCP server is built on this API and has no privileged path of its own. Anything it can do, your own code can do.

## What makes the answers usable

Most social tools will tell you a video has a lot of views. That is not the same as telling you something is happening, and the gap between the two is where bad decisions come from.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A score never arrives alone">
    Every momentum score ships with the confidence behind it, how many observations it rests on, how many hours those span, and how many comparable videos it was ranked against. A number with no coverage attached is a number you cannot defend in a meeting.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Claims are gated on evidence, not on size">
    A video is only classified as trending with at least **three metric snapshots across at least twelve hours**. Below that it is `watchlist` with a `gated_by` field explaining why — including when it has the largest view count in your corpus. One observation is not a trajectory.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Virality and trends are reported separately">
    One video outperforming is virality. A trend needs multiple independent creators moving together, and no single creator holding more than half the cluster's views. These are different findings and they are returned as different fields.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Views are never called impressions">
    Public video views are observed content views — potential exposures. Real ad impressions require first-party or Ads Manager data. Every response carrying view-derived numbers says so, because that sentence tends to get lost between an API and a board deck.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Missing is not zero">
    A metric that was never observed comes back as `null` with its coverage, never as `0`. An empty corpus is reported as an empty corpus, not as a result of zero.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
