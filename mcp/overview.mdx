Pepo runs as a [Model Context Protocol](https://modelcontextprotocol.io) server, so you can work through your video library inside Claude or ChatGPT, in the tool your team already uses. You ask, the assistant looks, you follow up: the way you would brainstorm with an analyst who has watched every video.

```
What do people complain about in the knee sleeve videos?
Now just the ones that show it being worn: is it different?
Which creators keep coming back to it?
```

The assistant calls Pepo, gets answers with the evidence behind them, and replies with links you can open, the clip each claim came from, and a cost line you can see.

Twenty-one of the tools also render an interactive panel in the conversation (answers with their evidence, trend board, video, share of voice, competitor, product, creators, campaign, dataset, collections and more) using the [MCP Apps extension](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/extensions/apps/overview). Where a host does not support that, the same information comes back as text and links. Nothing depends on the panel rendering.

## The design, in three rules

**Answers carry their own caveats.** Tool results state the confidence, the snapshot count, and whether a claim cleared its evidence bar, in the text the model reads first, not only in the structured payload. A result that has too little history to be called trending says so in a line the assistant will repeat, and a question no video could answer is refused with the reason.

**Errors carry a way forward.** Out of credits returns the largest version of your request that still fits your balance. No brand profile returns the setup path. An error that says only "no" ends the conversation.

**Volume returns links, not media.** At 25 or 1,000 videos nobody wants embedded players. One video under discussion gets a rendered panel; everything else is a table you can sort or a CSV you can take away.

## What it costs

The MCP server bills exactly like the API: same prepaid balance, same per-call ceilings. Paging through the library you already have is free. `check_credits` and `estimate_cost` are free and the assistant is told to use them before anything expensive, so you can see the price before agreeing to it.
